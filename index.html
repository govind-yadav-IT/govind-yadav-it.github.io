01 / ABOUT

Understanding what happens underneath the tools.

I’m Govind Yadav, a third-year BSc IT student building a foundation in cloud infrastructure, systems, automation, and AI infrastructure.

My work focuses on understanding infrastructure beyond simply using cloud tools. I build practical projects with AWS, Linux, Docker, Terraform, Git, GitHub Actions, Bash, and Nginx to explore how systems are provisioned, deployed, monitored, and maintained.

These projects cover infrastructure provisioning, containerization, CI/CD, automation, monitoring, logging, deployment workflows, and system operations. The goal is not to present tools as black boxes, but to understand the relationships between them and the engineering decisions behind a working system.
